    Cardputer users — grab the SD card files
    The Cardputer reads its SubGHz KeeLoq keys, NFC dictionaries, and IR remote databases from a microSD (the M5Stick bakes these into flash, so it doesn't need this). Extract this pack to the root of a FAT32 microSD and insert it — you'll get /subghz /nfc /infrared /Manifest. Without it, SubGHz decoding, NFC lookups and IR remotes won't work.

    How it works

    Select → build → flash

    01

    Pick your apps

    Tick the tools you want and choose StickS3 or Cardputer. Your selection only narrows the safe set — nothing you shouldn't have gets added.

    02

    We compile it

    A build runs in the cloud and produces a firmware with exactly your apps (~5–12 min). No account needed.

    03

    Flash in the browser

    When it's ready, plug in over USB in Chrome or Edge and hit Flash — it installs straight to the device.

    Why compile instead of drag-and-drop: the Cardputer has no PSRAM, so apps can't be loaded at runtime — they're built into the firmware. A custom build is how you get exactly the set you picked.
    Live serial

    Device console

    Watch your board's serial output live — boot messages, app output, and debug logs stream here while it's plugged in. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
