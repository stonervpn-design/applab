    The honest bit

    How installing works

    01

    Plug in over USB

    Connect the StickS3 or Cardputer with a data USB-C cable. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop — they speak Web Serial. (Safari and phones can't flash.)

    02

    Flash in the browser

    Hit Flash on your board, pick the serial port, and it installs the firmware straight to the device — every app in the catalog comes with it.

    03

    Open apps on the device

    Reboot into the menu and launch any tool. Your captures and dumps live on the device storage.

    Why bundles, not one-app-at-a-time: the Cardputer has no PSRAM, so it can't load apps at runtime — the apps are compiled into the firmware. One flash gets you the whole set.
